# Experimental Protocol

## Identification target

The hidden objective vector is nonnegative and normalized to sum to one. This resolves positive-scale ambiguity but does not guarantee unique coefficient recovery. The benchmark therefore prioritizes held-out decision regret over raw coefficient error.

## Training observations

Each training observation contains a capacity vector and the corresponding forward-optimal production decision under the same hidden objective. Capacity scenarios vary across observations so the active constraint geometry changes and reveals preference information.

## Inverse solver

The inverse master starts from a neutral uniform prior. Challenger constraints are generated by repeatedly solving the forward LP under the current candidate objective. Observation-specific nonnegative slacks allow inconsistent data without making the inverse master infeasible.

## Evaluation

The recovered objective, uniform baseline and a fixed random objective are each used to solve unseen capacity scenarios. Decisions are evaluated under the true hidden objective.

Primary metric: held-out decision regret.

Secondary metrics: decision L1 error, cosine similarity, objective L1 error, challenger count, iteration count and training slack.

## Acceptance rules

1. Forward decisions must satisfy resource and bound constraints.
2. Recovered objective weights must be nonnegative and sum to one.
3. Constraint generation stops only when no challenger violates inverse optimality beyond tolerance.
4. Test capacities are never used by the inverse solver.
5. Decision regret is computed under the true hidden objective, not the recovered objective.
6. Coefficient similarity alone is insufficient evidence of successful inverse optimization.
7. Baseline and negative results are retained.
